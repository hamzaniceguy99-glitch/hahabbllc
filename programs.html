Our services, in detail

Four services, with repair offered before replacement and recalibration taken seriously.

Program 01 · Recalibration included

Windshield replacement

On most vehicles built in the last decade, a camera behind the windshield runs lane keeping and automatic braking. Replacing the glass moves it, and the system must be recalibrated or it is working from the wrong reference. We tell you up front whether we do it in house or send it to a dealer.

  • 2 to 4 hours
  • Any vehicle
  • Quoted in writing
  • Recalibration space

Program 02 · Cheaper, where it still works

Chip & crack repair

A chip caught early is a thirty-minute repair that keeps your factory glass and your original camera calibration. Once it runs into the driver line of sight or past a certain length it is a replacement. We will tell you honestly which side of that line you are on.

  • 30 to 60 minutes
  • Repairable damage
  • Quoted in writing
  • Dry conditions

Program 03 · Including the clear-up

Side & rear glass

Tempered glass shatters into thousands of fragments that end up in door cavities, seat rails and carpet. Replacing the glass is the quick part; getting the debris out so it does not reappear for months is the part that separates a proper job from a fast one.

  • 1 to 2 hours
  • Any vehicle
  • Quoted in writing
  • Access to the vehicle

Program 04 · Weather permitting

Mobile service

Convenient and not always possible. Adhesive and resin both have temperature and humidity limits, and a windshield bonded in a downpour is not a safe windshield. We will reschedule or ask you to come in rather than fit in conditions the product does not allow.

  • Per visit
  • Any vehicle
  • Quoted in writing
  • Dry, level space
